# Fonts

The app ships two tiny subsets of Google Fonts, each holding only the glyphs it shows:

- `font-brush.woff2`: **Ma Shan Zheng** by Ma ShanZheng, the "Mahj Helper" title.
- `font-hanzi.woff2`: **Noto Serif SC** by Google, the 中 發 dragon tiles and the 麻將 seal.

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org
