For four players, following the National Mah Jongg League's usual rules. Many tables have their own house rules.

The tiles

An American mahjong set has 152 tiles.

  • Three suits, numbered 1 to 9, four of each: Bams, Craks and Dots.
  • Winds: North, East, West, South. Four of each.
  • Dragons: Red goes with Craks, Green goes with Bams, and White goes with Dots. White is called Soap, and it also stands for 0 in year hands like 2026.
  • Flowers: 8 of them. Any flower matches any other.
  • Jokers: 8 of them.

How you win

You win by making exactly one of the hands on the card, all 14 tiles. You hold 13 tiles, and you win on the 14th. The card changes every year, so buy the new one from the National Mah Jongg League each spring.

Hands are built from groups:

  • Pair: 2 of the same tile.
  • Pung: 3 of the same. Kong: 4 of the same. Quint: 5 of the same.
  • Singles: groups like NEWS or 2026, one of each tile.

Jokers

A joker can stand in for any tile in a pung, kong or quint. A joker can never be used in a pair or in singles. Jokers can't be passed in the Charleston.

In this app, a tile with a 3Bam badge is a spot where one of your jokers fills in.

Exposed or concealed

Hands marked X (exposed) let you call other players' discards to finish a pung or kong. You put that group face up on your rack.

Hands marked C (concealed) must be built from tiles you pick yourself. The only discard you can take is the one that wins.

Using this helper

  • On My tiles, tap each tile you're holding.
  • The helper shows the hands you are closest to. “3 away” means you need 3 more of the right tiles.
  • Faded dashed tiles are ones you still need. Plan to keep tiles that fit your top hands.
  • Tiles that none of your best hands use are listed as good to pass in the Charleston, or to discard.
  • When you call a discard and put a group face up, tap Lock tiles I've exposed and pick those tiles. The helper then shows only hands that include those tiles, and works out what you still need from them plus your hidden tiles.
  • On My card, add the hands from your own card. It only takes a minute per hand, and they stay on this device.

Early in a game, keep two or three hands in mind. Pick one once you see which tiles come to you.

How to type a hand

Type each group from the card with a space between groups.

1–9Number tiles
a b cAfter a group: its suit. Same letter = same suit, different letters = different suits. Use a for black groups on the card, b for red, c for green.
FFlower
N E W SWinds
DThe dragon that matches the group's suit (DDDa)
R G 0Red dragon, Green dragon, Soap (white dragon)

Examples

FF 2222a 44b 66b 8888a
Two flowers, then 2s and 8s in one suit, 4s and 6s in another.

222a 0000 2222b 666a
A year hand. 0 is Soap.

FF 1111a 2222b 3333c with "any numbers" ticked
Any three numbers in a row, one in each suit.
